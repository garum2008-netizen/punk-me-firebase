# Chapter 1 storyboard — for owner approval

Ten frames. The six pictures are the plates in [art/](art/). They are proposals, not screenshots of the current game. The Russian script is the source of the words.

| # | Beat | What the child sees | What is said | Picture |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | Invitation | The band stands in front of the night houses. Big line: join the band. | “One night. House 13. Earn the patch.” Angelina introduces herself. | [01-title.png](art/01-title.png) |
| 2 | Your room | House 13. Angelina by the wall, you by the lamp. Goal at the top: walk the room. | “Walk to the yellow marker.” Why: the street door stays shut until you can follow it. | [03-house13.png](art/03-house13.png) |
| 3 | The door | The street button and the door, with the same goal sentence. | “The patch is not in the room.” | Uses the street plate’s doorway, frame 4. |

| 5 | Practice yard | You and Razor, a short chain between you, stones and pumpkin lamps. No fright. | “Press Chain. Nobody gets hurt. There is no patch yet.” | [05-cemetery.png](art/05-cemetery.png) |
| 6 | After practice | The tracker says step 6, not “chapter complete.” | “Razor is still waiting.” | Same yard, then the shop button. |
| 7 | The pin | The shop, the free pin highlighted, a line that says coins are not a real card. | “Press Shop. The pin is free.” | Shop UI on top of the street plate. |

Frame 1 is the thumbnail. Frames 2, 4, 5, 9, and 10 are the backgrounds a child should be able to name without help: room, street, practice yard, square, climax.

Nothing in frames 5 or 10 is a wound, a weapon against a person, or a jump scare. The chain is the band’s sport.

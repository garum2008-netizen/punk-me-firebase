# Key-art sources

These six PNGs are pre-production plates for owner approval. They are not in the playable build. Each file is 1920×1080.

Plate titles are Russian. A short English and Danish line sits under the Russian subtitle. In House 13, pink Angelina stands by the poster wall and the blue-haired player stands by the lamp. The caption says the same thing.

They were rendered in the browser from `render.html` in this folder, with the local Three.js scene and no image API. No Adopt Me poster and no other studio’s name or asset was used. The captions were drawn in the page.

| File | Scene | What was used |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `01-title.png` | Title, the band in front of the night houses | `punk_pink.glb`, `razor.glb`, `cobalt.glb`, `mohawk.glb`, windowed houses, awnings, doors, a «ДОМ 13» sign, fences, crates, lamp posts, pumpkins |
| `02-pumpkingrad-skyline.png` | Pumpkingrad, one stall, dark towers | `cobalt.glb`, `punk_pink.glb`, windowed towers, a striped stall with jars and a «ЛАРЁК» sign, crates, lamp posts, pumpkins |
| `03-house13.png` | House 13. Angelina by the wall, the player by the lamp | `punk_pink.glb` by the poster wall, `cobalt.glb` by the floor lamp, side walls, rug, shelf and books, bed, window, lamp |
| `04-street-razor.png` | Razor's yard on the street | `cobalt.glb`, `punk_pink.glb`, `razor.glb`, two windowed houses, a «РЕЙЗОР» sign, fences, crates, lamp posts, pumpkins |
| `05-cemetery.png` | The practice yard | `cobalt.glb`, `razor.glb`, waist-high chain links, a path, an iron fence, rounded stones, trees, lamp posts, pumpkins |
| `06-climax.png` | The real duel, the stall, the patch | `cobalt.glb`, `razor.glb`, the striped stall and «ЛАРЁК» sign, windowed towers, lamp posts, a small gold disc for the patch |

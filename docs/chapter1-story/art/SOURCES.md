# Key-art sources

These six PNGs are pre-production plates for owner approval. They are not in the playable build. Each file is 1920×1080.

They were rendered in the browser from `render.html` in this folder, with the local Three.js scene and no image API. No Adopt Me poster and no other studio’s name or asset was used. The captions were drawn in the page.

| File | Scene | What was used |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `01-title.png` | Title, the band in front of the night houses | `punk_pink.glb`, `razor.glb`, `cobalt.glb`, `mohawk.glb`, plus boxes, a window texture, and two pumpkins made in `render.html` |
| `02-pumpkingrad-skyline.png` | Pumpkingrad, one stall, dark towers | `cobalt.glb`, `punk_pink.glb`, box towers, a stall, pumpkins, a moon sphere |
| `03-house13.png` | House 13, Angelina and the player by the lamp | `punk_pink.glb` as Angelina, `cobalt.glb` as the player, a wall, a poster plane, a bed box, a lamp |
| `04-street-razor.png` | The street, Razor keeping the patch | `cobalt.glb`, `punk_pink.glb`, `razor.glb`, two buildings, a pink sign, one pumpkin |
| `05-cemetery.png` | The practice chain duel | `cobalt.glb`, `razor.glb`, torus links, low stones, two pumpkins |
| `06-climax.png` | The real duel, the stall, the patch | `cobalt.glb`, `razor.glb`, torus links, the stall, two dark towers, a small gold disc for the patch |
